Dark theme palette prototypes

Three dark UI themes designed for long-form notes, code editors, and terminals. Each avoids pure black and pure white, keeps large surfaces low-contrast, and reserves saturated colors for orientation, emphasis, and active states.

Kanagawa • Hokusai • Shogun

Ukiyo Night

indigo ink, weathered paper, wave foam, muted vermilion, antique gold

Research note title

Plain text should feel soft, stable, and readable. Use bold emphasis for important claims, and use highlighting sparingly for things you need to find again.

The best dark themes avoid pure black, pure white, and over-saturated accents in long text.
